# Sales Performance & Commercial Insights with Tableau (ST2187)

## Overview
This repository presents a business analytics case study completed for the University of London module ST2187 - Business Analytics, Applied Modelling and Prediction.

Using a real-world sales dataset, the project applies Tableau data visualisation techniques to explore trends, patterns, and relationships in sales, profit, customers, products, and geography. The goal is to extract commercially meaningful insights and present data-driven recommendations to senior management.

## Business Objective
The project simulates a real-life business analytics scenario where analysts are required to:
- Explore raw data without predefined questions
- Identify key trends, risks, and growth opportunities
- Translate visual insights into actionable recommendations

## Analytical Scope
The Tableau story is structured into five analytical themes:
- Sales and Profit Analysis
- Product Performance Analysis
- Customer Sales Insights
- Geographical Analysis
- Forecast and What-If Analysis

## Key Insights
### Sales and Profit Trends
- Overall sales and profit showed a strong upward trend from 2019 to 2022
- Clear seasonality observed, with consistent dips in Q1
- Consumer segment emerged as the largest contributor to total sales

### Product Performance
- Technology products were the highest-performing category by sales
- Copiers generated the highest profit, while tables were loss-making despite strong sales
- High discount rates were identified as a key driver of product losses

### Customer Insights
- Consumer segment dominated sales across all customer groups
- Returning customers accounted for the majority of revenue
- Opportunity identified to strengthen new customer acquisition strategies

### Geographical Insights
- APAC identified as the fastest-growing and highest-sales market
- Canada showed high profit margins despite low total sales
- Certain countries and cities consistently incurred losses

### Forecast & What-If Analysis
- Sales and profit forecasts indicate continued growth
- What-if analysis showed that reducing discount rates could turn loss-making products profitable without reducing sales volume

## Tools Used 
- Tableau for data visualisation and dashboard creation

## Skills Demonstrated
- Data exploration and visual storytelling
- Dashboard design for executive decision-making
- Commercial insight generation from raw data
- Forecasting and scenario (what-if) analysis
